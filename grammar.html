01

Nouns

Case tells you the noun's job.

Nouns change for number and case. The workbook also assigns a feminine, masculine, or mixed gender/class pattern, which selects the endings.

Two dimensions

Number
Singular for one; plural for more than one.
Case
The noun's role: subject, object, recipient, origin, place, means, or possession.

02

Verbs

Person, time, and view select the form.

Verb paradigms combine six grammatical persons with tense and aspect. The verb "to be" has its own recorded table.

1SGIfirst singular
2SGyousecond singular
3SGhe / she / itthird singular
1PLwefirst plural
2PLyousecond plural
3PLtheythird plural

Tense

When?

  • Present - now, generally, or repeatedly
  • Future - after now
  • Past - before now
  • Irrealis - imagined, conditional, or not presented as fact

Aspect

How is the action viewed?

  • Non-perfective - ongoing, repeated, or open-ended
  • Perfective - viewed as a completed whole

These are reading aids; the generated forms still require linguistic review.

05

Dictionary notation

Know what the interface is showing.

INT / DEU

Two Roman spellings: international and German-oriented. Noun and verb paradigms have corresponding variants.

Square brackets

Supplementary information attached to a lemma, flexion field, or source. Parentheses belong to supplementary meaning information.
