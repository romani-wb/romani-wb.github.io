A short context

A documented language with a living history

01

Place and contact

Roman is the name Burgenland Roma use for their language. It is a Romani variety shaped in the former western Hungarian region and influenced over time by German and Croatian.

02

Pressure and continuity

Persecution, the Holocaust, persistent discrimination, and post-war language shift sharply reduced everyday use. Roman nevertheless remains an important marker of community and identity.

03

Codification and access

Community self-organisation led to codification work with linguists from the University of Graz. Teaching, media, Roma-Service activities, and this dictionary continue that work.

Roman has been listed in the Austrian UNESCO Commission’s national inventory of intangible cultural heritage since 2011.
